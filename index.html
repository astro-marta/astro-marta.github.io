01 / THE PERSON+
KIČEVO
MK ROOTED IN KIČEVO

A SMALL TOWN. AN EXPANSIVE OUTLOOK.

The path to space
starts with people.

I grew up in Kičevo, North Macedonia. Building a life in space exploration meant finding a path across disciplines, countries, and expectations.

Today, I bring together materials engineering, space studies, and experience in isolated mission environments. As a Program Human Systems Discipline Integrator through Leidos, I support NASA Johnson Space Center’s work in Human Health and Performance and the Extravehicular Activity and Human Surface Mobility Program.

Alongside that work, I founded the International Space Alliance to help people and communities find their own way into the space sector. Access to exploration should be as ambitious as exploration itself.
