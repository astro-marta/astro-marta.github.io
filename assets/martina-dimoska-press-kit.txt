MARTINA DIMOSKA — SHORT BIO & PRESS NOTES
Prepared October 2026

SHORT BIO
Martina Dimoska is a Macedonian analog astronaut, engineer, and founder and president of the International Space Alliance. She has commanded the SELENE and TERRA NOVA analog missions at LunAres Research Station in Poland, served as science officer on the University of North Dakota's ILMAH 14 mission, and participated as crew engineer and outreach lead in the APICES mission in Spain. Through Leidos, she supports NASA Johnson Space Center as a Program Human Systems Discipline Integrator. Her outreach focuses on expanding participation in space, especially for communities without established space industries.

SELECTED TOPICS

- Building pathways into space from emerging space regions
- Science communication and space education

ACCURACY NOTES
Analog missions are conducted on Earth and simulate aspects of space exploration. They are not spaceflights. Martina supports NASA through Leidos; this personal website does not imply NASA endorsement.
