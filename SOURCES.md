# Content sources and image credits

Research date: 28 October 2026. The website is written as personal portfolio copy, with factual claims grounded in the sources below. Update roles, dates, and affiliations when they change.

## Biography, education, and professional work

- [Wikipedia: Martina Dimoska](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martina_Dimoska) — starting point for biography, education, mission history, and further references.
- [NMD: Official NASA portrait and human systems work](https://nmd.mk/golemo-me%D1%93unarodno-priznanie-za-makedoni%D1%98a-oficzi%D1%98alen-nasa-portret-na-martina-dimoska/) — Program Human Systems Discipline Integrator, working through Leidos to support NASA Johnson Space Center, HHP, and EHP.
- [N2K T-Minus: The Balkans Join the Space Conversation](https://space.n2k.com/podcasts/t-minus/ds63) — first-person interview covering background, education, the International Space Alliance, and access to the space sector. Published 31 August 2024.
- [Florida Tech, College of Engineering and Science newsletter, Fall 2025](https://www.fit.edu/media/pprdocs/coes/20250825_FINAL-WEB_COES-Newsletter-Fall-2025---accessible.pdf) — alumni profile and analog mission experience.

The education section describes Kent State as an exchange and Florida Tech as a graduate certificate, not degree programs. The ISU entry uses “Master’s studies” because public sources vary between enrollment and completion language. Confirm the preferred degree wording before changing it to an awarded degree.

## Analog missions

- [LunAres mission archive](https://lunares.space/missions-archive/) — TERRA NOVA (Analog Axiom), commander, 3–16 June 2025; SELENE, commander, 26 August–8 September 2023. The separate Analog ISS crew is not attributed to Martina.
- [LunAres ambassadors](https://lunares.space/lunares-ambassadors/) — International Space Alliance founder/president and SELENE commander.
- [LunAres AstroMentalHealth project](https://lunares.space/astromentalhealth-terra-nova-mission/) — project context. The website makes no claims about published findings.
- [University of North Dakota, “On the ground at UND but 63.4 million miles away”](https://blogs.und.edu/und-today/2022/10/on-the-ground-at-und-but-63-4-million-miles-away/) — ILMAH 14 began 17 October 2022, Martina served as science officer; spacesuit, exercise, drone, plant research, and a 20-minute one-way simulated communications delay. Published 20 October 2022. The planned maximum duration is not represented as a completed mission duration.
- [ESA BIC Czech Republic: APICES mission announcement](https://www.esa-bic.cz/news/apices-mission/) — announcement published 21 July 2023. Role and June 2023 mission context also cross-checked with the biography and NMD reporting.

All four missions took place on Earth. “Analog astronaut” is used consistently; the site does not claim orbital flight or membership in NASA’s astronaut corps. Mission diagrams are original decorative illustrations, not geographic maps or hardware schematics.
